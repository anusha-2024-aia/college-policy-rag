Under KVIT Academic Regulations 2024 v2.4, regular degree candidates must maintain a minimum of 80% aggregate attendance in each registered course [1].

In cases of certified medical illness or approved university athletics participation, a condonation of up to 10% may be granted by the Academic Dean [2].

Students with attendance between 65% and 74% are placed on Academic Probation and must undertake mandatory remedial coursework prior to the following session [3]. Students falling strictly below 65% are barred from final semester examinations without appeal [1].
